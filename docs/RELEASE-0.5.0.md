# TaleForge 0.5.0

Editing a character’s basics no longer loses progress. Ability score increases from level-ups are kept when you change a creation score, the score method, or background boosts, and the builder shows how much each final score has grown since creation. The Elf Keen Senses or Human Skillful skill now survives reloads, so later edits no longer ask for it again or leave a duplicate proficiency.

Character creation is clearer. Elf, Gnome, Tiefling, Dragonborn, and Goliath characters choose their 2024 lineage, legacy, or ancestry from a list, and Wood Elf sets a 35-foot speed. Languages start blank with Common fixed and are grouped into Standard and Rare languages. Point Buy enforces scores of 8–15 and exactly 27 points, Manual scores must be whole numbers, class skill lists hide skills your background or species already grant, and the origin step lists every unfinished choice at once. Backgrounds are alphabetical, and Soldier asks for its gaming set.

Rules corrections: Resistance now halves damage (rounding down) before Vulnerability doubles it, following the 2024 order of application. Cleric Channel Divinity reaches four uses at level 18, and Monk Unarmored Movement stacks with Barbarian Fast Movement. Resources & rest adds counters for Orc Relentless Endurance, Goliath Giant Ancestry, Dragonborn Draconic Flight, Elf and Tiefling lineage spells, Forest Gnome Speak with Animals, and Monk Uncanny Metabolism. Human Resourceful now marks Heroic Inspiration as available after a Long Rest, replacing the separate Resourceful counter.

On the sheet and roster, **Print sheet** prints the character or saves it as a PDF, and **Duplicate** copies a character, for example to try another build. Roster cards list every class with its subclass. Browser tabs show the character and page, the spellbook is sorted by level, removing a journal entry with text asks first, and number fields such as ability scores, hit points, and coins can be cleared and retyped. On phones, the top bar is shorter, roster actions form a 2 × 2 grid, and the skip link appears only for keyboard users.

Existing Wood Elf sheets keep their saved speed: re-pick the lineage in **Edit basics** or change the speed in Setup.

Stop the previous host, extract the new ZIP, and launch **Start TaleForge Host.bat**. Keep the same Windows account, browser, address and port to retain saved-sheet and room access. Campaigns continue using the existing host data folder. Nothing needs to be installed by players.
