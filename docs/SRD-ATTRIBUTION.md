The spell, feat, class, subclass, and species catalogs were obtained from the Open5e v2 API, filtered to document key srd-2024, on November 5, 2026. Content is stored locally for offline LAN use. Display fields were simplified; spell descriptions retain their source text. The Draconic Sorcery Dragon Companion feature’s level was corrected from 19 in that dataset to 18, matching the official 2024 rules.

The Aasimar traits and origin feats absent from the SRD catalog use concise, original reference summaries. References used to check those summaries:

- [2024 species overview](https://www.dndbeyond.com/posts/1783-the-10-species-in-the-2024-players-handbook) and [Aasimar reference](https://www.wargamer.com/dnd/aasimar).
- [2024 backgrounds and origin feats](https://www.dndbeyond.com/posts/1785-the-backgrounds-and-origin-feats-in-the-2024), including Healer, Lucky, Musician, and Tavern Brawler.
- [Crafter reference and Fast Crafting table](https://roll20.net/compendium/dnd5e/Feats:Crafter?expansion=32231).
- [2024 rules glossary](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/br-2024/rules-glossary) for local rules reminders.

Species traits are displayed individually and filtered by character-level unlocks. Lineage tables and referenced spells remain available inside the feature description. Saved custom descriptions take precedence when resolving added features; unknown homebrew choices need a user-provided description.

Additional automation and descriptions are original implementation work. The full catalog describes many effects that are resolved manually; catalog inclusion does not imply complete automation.
