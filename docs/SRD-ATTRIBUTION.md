The spell, feat, class, and subclass catalogs were obtained from the Open5e v2 API, filtered to document key srd-2024, on November 5, 2026. Content is stored locally for offline LAN use. Display fields were simplified; spell descriptions retain their source text. The Draconic Sorcery Dragon Companion feature’s level was corrected from 19 in that dataset to 18, matching the official 2024 rules.

Additional automation and descriptions are original implementation work. The full catalog describes many effects that are resolved manually; catalog inclusion does not imply complete automation.
